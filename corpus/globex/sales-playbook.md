# Globex sales playbook

Qualification comes before demonstration. A prospect is qualified when the buying role, the
budget owner, and the timeline are known. Demonstrations before qualification are declined.

Discovery calls follow a fixed set of questions covering the current process, the cost of the
current process, and the decision criteria. The answers are recorded in the account record the
same day.

Proposals use the standard template and are reviewed by a second seller before they are sent.
Discounts beyond the published schedule require the regional lead's approval.
