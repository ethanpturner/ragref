# Globex staff handbook

Globex is organised into delivery teams, each owning a product area end to end. Teams set their
own working hours within the core hours of ten to three in the team's home time zone.

Communication defaults to written and asynchronous. Meetings are for decisions that need a
conversation, and every meeting produces a written note that goes to the team channel.

Every employee has a development budget for training and conferences, renewed annually. Requests
go to the team lead and are approved unless the budget is exhausted.

The handbook is revised twice a year. Proposals for changes are made by pull request against the
handbook repository, and anyone can propose one.
