# Globex pricing model

List prices are set per seat per month with three tiers. The tiers differ in the number of
connected sources, the retention window, and the level of support.

Enterprise agreements are priced on committed seats with a true-up at each anniversary. The
true-up uses the peak seat count in the preceding year, not the count on the anniversary date.

Regional multipliers apply to list prices and are revised annually against the published index.
The multiplier table is confidential to the pricing team and the regional leads.

Partner resale carries a fixed margin on list price. Partners may not discount below the floor
without written approval from the head of partnerships.
