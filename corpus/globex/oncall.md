# Globex on-call procedure

Each delivery team runs its own rotation with a primary and a secondary. The primary acknowledges
a page within fifteen minutes; the secondary is paged automatically if the primary does not.

Incidents are declared in the incident channel with a severity, an owner, and a one-line summary.
Severity one incidents open a bridge call and page the duty manager.

Every incident above severity three produces a written review within five working days. The
review is blameless and lists the timeline, the contributing factors, and the follow-up actions.

Handover between rotations happens in writing. The outgoing primary lists open alerts, silenced
alerts, and anything in progress.
