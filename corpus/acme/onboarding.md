# Acme onboarding guide

Welcome to Acme. Your first week covers accounts, equipment, and the reading list. Your manager
schedules the introductions; the people team schedules the training.

Accounts are provisioned by the identity team within one business day of your start date. The
request is raised automatically from the HR record, so nothing needs to be filed by hand. If an
account is missing on day two, ask your manager to check the record rather than raising a ticket.

Equipment ships to the address on file. A laptop, a monitor, and a headset are standard. Anything
else is requested through the equipment form and approved by the cost centre owner.

The reading list is short on purpose: the engineering handbook, the incident process, and the
security policy summary. Read them in that order.
