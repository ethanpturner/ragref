# Acme benefits summary

Health cover starts on the first day of employment. Dependants can be added within thirty days of
the start date or within thirty days of a qualifying life event.

The pension contribution matches employee contributions up to six percent of base salary. Changes
to the contribution rate take effect from the next payroll run after the change is submitted.

Leave accrues monthly. Unused leave carries over to the following year up to a cap of ten days;
anything above the cap is paid out at year end at the base daily rate.
