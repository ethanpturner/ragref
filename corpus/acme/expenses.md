# Acme expense policy

Expenses are submitted within thirty days of being incurred. Receipts are required for any item
over fifteen currency units. Items without a receipt above that threshold are reimbursed only with
the approval of the cost centre owner and a written explanation.

Travel is booked through the travel desk. Self-booked travel is reimbursed at the fare the travel
desk would have obtained, not at the fare paid, which is the rule that catches most people.

Meals while travelling are capped per day by region. The cap table is maintained by finance and
revised each January. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any heading.

Corporate cards are issued to staff who travel more than four times a year. Card statements are
reconciled monthly and an unreconciled statement suspends the card.
