# Acme control inventory

This document lists the controls in scope for the annual review and the owner of each.

Access control: quarterly access reviews for production systems, owned by the platform lead.
Evidence is the signed review record and the list of removed grants.

Change management: every production change goes through a reviewed pull request and a deployment
record. Evidence is the deployment log cross-referenced with the change tickets.

Logging: production logs are retained for one year and are write-once for the first ninety days.
Evidence is the retention configuration export and a sampled restore.

Vendor management: critical vendors are reviewed annually against the vendor questionnaire.
Evidence is the completed questionnaire and the risk rating.
