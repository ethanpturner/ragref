# Acme audit findings, 2026

Three findings were raised in the 2026 review. Two are closed and one remains open.

Finding one, closed: the quarterly access review for the reporting warehouse was not performed in
the first quarter. The review was completed in the second quarter and a calendar control added.

Finding two, closed: two service accounts held standing write access to the deployment system
without a documented owner. Owners were assigned and the access reduced to deploy-time only.

Finding three, open: log retention for one auxiliary system is configured at ninety days rather
than one year. Remediation is scheduled with the storage migration and tracked by the platform
lead.
